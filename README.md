# Rabia Khan | Professional Portfolio

A responsive professional portfolio website showcasing my background across software engineering, Agile delivery, data analytics, technical training, and mentorship.

The portfolio was designed to bring together my technical experience, career progression, professional competencies, education, certifications, and community impact in one interactive experience.

## About the Project

This portfolio was created to provide recruiters, hiring managers, collaborators, and professional connections with a clear overview of my experience and technical capabilities.

Rather than functioning as a static resume, the website presents my career journey through interactive sections that allow visitors to explore different areas of my background.

The site highlights:

- Software engineering experience
- Scrum Master and Agile delivery experience
- Data analytics and reporting
- Business technical training
- Technical mentorship
- Education and certifications
- Professional tools and technologies
- Contact information

## Key Features

### Career Evolution

The Career Evolution section provides an interactive timeline of my professional development.

Visitors can select different career stages to explore:

1. Software Engineering
2. Scrum Master / Agile Delivery
3. Digital Marketing & Analytics
4. Technical Mentorship
5. Business Technical Training

Each stage dynamically updates the description, timeframe, and key contributions without requiring the page to reload.

### Professional Experience

The Professional Experience section presents individual experience cards for:

- Genesys Works
- Weight Watchers
- P1Games
- SEO
- Acadium

Each experience includes a detailed modal that allows visitors to view additional responsibilities and accomplishments.

### Data & Analytics

The portfolio highlights practical analytics experience including:

- Microsoft Excel
- Google Analytics
- LinkedIn Analytics
- Instagram Insights
- Email performance metrics
- KPI tracking
- Performance reporting
- Dashboard reporting
- Data validation
- Business intelligence fundamentals

The goal is to demonstrate how technical and business skills can be combined to support data-informed decisions.

### Core Competencies

Skills are organized into four categories:

- Data & Analytics
- Software & Technology
- Agile & Delivery
- Tools & Enablement

This organization allows visitors to quickly identify the areas most relevant to a role or project.

### Responsive Navigation

The website includes a responsive navigation system that adapts to desktop and mobile screen sizes.

On smaller screens, visitors can open a mobile navigation menu to move between sections.

### Contact Form

The portfolio includes a contact form where visitors can enter:

- Name
- Email
- Message or inquiry

The current implementation provides an interactive confirmation message after submission.
